Privacy Policy for Aura Royale

Last updated: July 2024

TL;DR: Aura Royale doesn't collect your personal data, but AdMob (our ad provider) uses standard advertising identifiers for ads. You can reset this ID in your device settings.

1. Information We Don't Collect

Aura Royale does not collect, store, or share any personal information such as:

  • Your name or email address
  • Contacts or messages
  • Location data
  • Payment information

2. AdMob Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (resettable in device settings)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location (country-level from IP address)
  • Ad performance data (clicks, impressions)

This data is used to:

  • Show relevant ads
  • Measure ad effectiveness
  • Prevent fraud

Learn more about Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Privacy Info

3. App Permissions

Aura Royale requires:

  • INTERNET - For loading ads and game content
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - To check connection status

4. Your Choices

You can:

  • Reset your advertising ID in Android Settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads in Google Account settings
  • Contact us to delete any data (though we don't store user data)

5. Changes to This Policy

We'll notify users of significant changes by:

  • Updating this page
  • In-app notifications (if applicable)
